Final design

The Cinema Sprocket Scalloped Wordmark

An authentic cinema ticket pass with 3 top & bottom sprocket scallops, dashed perforation cut, and Sunset Saffron play triangle.

  • Replaces jagged paper tears with authentic 35mm cinema reel scallops
  • Maximized ticket footprint on app icons (~80% canvas coverage)
  • Zero extraneous dots: clean, uncluttered, high-impact breathing space
  • Seamless visual flow: get (charcoal) -> [my ▶] ticket -> show (electric indigo)
